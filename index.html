ACC · KAMIKAT

Privacy policy

ACC is a private tool made for one person: Kami (the creator behind the Kamikat and KamiKatPlus channels). It runs only on his own computer and is not offered to the public.

What ACC accesses

When Kami connects his own YouTube channels, ACC uses Google's official APIs to read, with read-only access:

  • channel and video information (YouTube Data API);
  • channel statistics and revenue reports (YouTube Analytics API).

Where the data goes

Nowhere else. The data and the sign-in tokens are stored only on Kami's computer. ACC has no server, does not share, sell or transfer data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

Google API Services

ACC's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in ACC (Settings → Google → ×) or in the Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing it deletes nothing on YouTube.
